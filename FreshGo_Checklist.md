# FreshGo report: verification checklist

Do these in order. If a source cannot be confirmed, remove it and the claim it supports.

| # | Check | Why it matters | If it fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | FreshGo brief: reread every "(brief)" claim | The brief is the only evidence for FreshGo. Claims it does not make are invented facts | Mark as an assumption or remove |
| 2 | ONS "online is about 10% of food retail sales": open the ONS release | The figure came from an earlier draft, not a confirmed source. Use a food or grocery measure, not the all-retail share | Use the figure the release gives, or delete the clause |
| 3 | Ocado "99% of items delivered as promised": find it in Ocado Retail's Annual Report 2025 | Not yet confirmed, and the earlier "trading statement, January 2025" source may be wrong | Use the exact wording and number from the report, or describe fulfilment reliability without a percentage |
| 4 | RTE (2026) North American partners closing centres: open the article | It is the evidence for "do not copy Ocado wholesale" | Drop the closures claim and rely on capital intensity |
| 5 | Ocado "15.8% growth, 2.2% share": find the Worldpanel by Numerator release | Currently sourced to news coverage, not the primary release | Cite the release, or attribute it to the news source |
| 6 | DeHoratius and Raman (2008), Fisher (1997), Lee et al. (1997): open each and check year and claim | Cited from memory | Correct the details, or cut the citation and its claim |
| 7 | Assignment rules: word limit, whether the table counts, reference list, title page, cover sheet, AI declaration | Decides what you must submit and how it is counted | Add what is required, and cut words if the table counts |

## Keep labelled as assumptions

- Dispatch and driver capacity saturate first.
- Manual picking and layout create the bottleneck.
- The "two peak periods" test.
- The 30% stress case.

## Before you submit

- Add a reference list with one entry for every source cited.
- Read the report once aloud and rewrite anything that is not in your own words.
