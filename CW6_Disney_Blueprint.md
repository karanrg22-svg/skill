## 2. THE CORE ARGUMENT (golden thread)

### 2.1 Chosen technology (one only)
**AI/machine-learning-driven personalisation (recommendation & discovery engine)** — *with an editorial governance layer.*
Why this over alternatives (justify in notes + appendix A1 scoring table):
| Option | Strategic fit to brief's challenges | Maturity/time-to-value | Org-impact richness (AC2) | Ethical/regulatory complexity (AC3) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ML personalisation** | Directly hits "customer data & analytics to improve engagement and recommendations" **and** the creative-vs-data tension | High (proven at Netflix/Amazon/Spotify) | Very high (creative, data, compliance all affected) | High, rich (children, bias, GDPR) | **Chosen** |
| Generative AI for content production | Hits cost/content-production challenge | Medium; legal/IP uncertainty | High but dominated by labour/IP litigation | Very high | Rejected: too contested; risks becoming a labour-law essay |
| Process automation/RPA | Hits operations only | High | Low | Low | Rejected: weak on LO3 advantage |

### 2.2 One-sentence thesis (put on slide 1 and slide 11)
> **"Machine-learning personalisation will only create *sustained* advantage for Disney+ if it is governed *with* its creative teams and aimed at the franchise flywheel — not just watch-time — because the algorithm itself is table stakes; the combination of IP, data, creative judgement and trust is not."**

### 2.3 The five original / critical moves (this is what separates 70s from 80s)
1. **Reframe the problem:** Disney+ doesn't lack content or data tech; it lacks *alignment* between creative, data and compliance (brief says views differ). Technology adoption is an *organisational* problem first.
2. **Reframe the objective:** Optimise **discovery breadth + lifetime value across the franchise ecosystem** (Marvel → Pixar → Star Wars → parks/merch → ESPN/Hulu), not raw engagement. Warn of the *engagement-optimisation trap* (Goodhart's law: when a measure becomes a target it ceases to be a good measure).
3. **Algorithm = parity, not advantage:** Using VRIN, show recommender algorithms are imitable; advantage comes from *complementary assets* (IP, cross-ecosystem data, creative-data fusion capability, trust with families).
4. **Resistance as information (Ford, Ford & D'Amelio):** Treat creative-team pushback as design input, then operationalise via a **Creative Veto + Editorial Override** — a concrete, rare, feasible governance mechanism.
5. **Ethics as strategy with measured trade-offs:** Children/family trust is Disney's most valuable intangible. Show guardrail KPIs (diversity of exposure, no behavioural profiling on kids' profiles) and *admit the short-term engagement cost*.
