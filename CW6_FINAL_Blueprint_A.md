## 2. THE ARGUMENT (lock this before touching PowerPoint)
| Element | Locked choice |
|---|---|
| **Technology (one)** | ML-driven personalisation (home rows, artwork, search, notifications, kids' profiles) **with an editorial governance layer** |
| **Problem** | Disney+ has scale and franchises but converts them to retention imperfectly: generic discovery in a low-switching-cost market, plus creative, data/marketing and operations/compliance teams with different priorities (all from the brief) |
| **Stakeholder (one)** | **Creative teams** |
| **Thesis (put on S1 and S11)** | Personalisation is table stakes; **sustained** advantage comes only from combining franchise IP, ecosystem data, creative judgement and family trust — governed *with* creative teams and aimed at **discovery breadth and franchise lifetime value**, not just watch-time |
| **Original angle (say it twice)** | Optimise for the **franchise ecosystem** (Marvel → Pixar → Star Wars → parks/merch → Hulu/ESPN), and warn against the engagement-optimisation trap (Goodhart's law) |
| **Three recommendations** | **R1** Creative–Data Governance Council (govern first) · **R2** Gated pilot-then-scale on a unified data foundation (prove, then scale) · **R3** Digital-confidence and hybrid-roles programme (build in parallel) |
| **Thread phrases** | "story-led", "data-informed", "trust-bound" |

**Five critical moves to land (these separate 70s from 80s):**
1. Reframe the problem as **alignment**, not lack of technology.
2. Reframe the objective: **discovery breadth + ecosystem value** vs watch-time.
3. **Algorithm = parity, not advantage**; advantage = complementary assets and capability.
4. **Resistance as information**: operationalise creative-team objections (veto + override).
5. **Ethics as strategy with admitted costs**: guardrail KPIs; short-term engagement trade-off.
