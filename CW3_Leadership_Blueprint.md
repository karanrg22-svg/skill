### 2.1 What a harsh marker LOVES
1. **Argument, not description**: every paragraph makes a claim, backs it with theory and evidence, and weighs a counter-view.
2. **Fit-for-purpose theory** with its limits stated (one clause each).
3. **Integration**: theories combined and compared, not listed.
4. **Conceptual precision** (e.g., differentiating power bases and influence tactics, distinguishing compliance from commitment).
5. **Evidence quality**: peer-reviewed literature plus credible, current real-world evidence; sources synthesised, not strung together.
6. **Nuanced judgement** (not "good/bad leader"): what was defensible, what wasn't, under what conditions.
7. **Trade-offs and uncertainty** named explicitly.
8. **Ownership** in AC4: honest, specific, evidence-based, with realistic actions.
9. **A coherent report**: the four parts talk to each other.

### 5.1 Scenario evidence map (use only what the scenario states; label inferences)
| What the scenario states | Concept it speaks to |
